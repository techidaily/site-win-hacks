## DEX 3 in 2018

Throughout 2017 we’ve not only released a series of substantial upgrades to our [best DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) product but have prepped our code for big things moving forward. Much of these upcoming additions are largely based on user feedback — what you, the DEX 3 DJ, wants to see included in future free updates.

[LEARN MORE ABOUT DEX 3 AND DOWNLOAD A FREE TRIAL ![DEX 3.9.0.10 DJ software screenshot](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/dex39010_screenshot.jpg?fit=300%2C169&ssl=1 "DEX 3.9.0.10 DJ software screenhot")](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)

After the addition of the integrated [Party Tyme Karaoke subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), not surprisingly, the requests have been pilling in for additional karaoke-specific features designed to make a karaoke hosts job easier. Notably: More rotation management, singer/song history, and other karaoke features that KJs have come to rely on with karaoke only software products such as [PCDJ Karaoki](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

We’re already deep into development on a few of these requested items, with the first implementation set to launch sometime in January. I will go out on a limb and say right now you will be impressed — [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) has rapidly morphed into the true all-in-one entertainment software solution for today’s versatile DJ/VJ/KJ and this year will further solidify that status.

Beyond karaoke, you’ll see improvements to some of the core features such as time-stretching, [DJ controller](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) support (including implementing all the latest DJ controllers released next year), browser/track management features, video mixing features, and more.

Prior users of the now defunct in-app Pulselocker music subscription should see a replacement on the horizon as well, with a few new players set to emerge that will provide a quality and commercially legal music subscription service for DJ software integration. We’re strong believers that streaming is the future for DJs (with offline caching support) so it’s a priority for us to provide a new solution DJs can truly count on.

There’s a lot more I simply can’t post about just yet as our competition is most certainly paying attention. [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) has emerged as a top solution for the modern DJ already and 2018 is shaping up to be a banner year for new and exciting innovations. _We owe it to you!_
