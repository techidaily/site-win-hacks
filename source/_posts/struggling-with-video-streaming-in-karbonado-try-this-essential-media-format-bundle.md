In most instances Windows-based computers already come equipped with the [video codecs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video%5Fcodec) required for Karaoki to play **MP4 karaoke video files**, but rarely for other video file formats and/or video containers. Third-party media software can also overwrite the pre-loaded **video codecs** and completely prevent Karaoki from playing **karaoke video files** in general.

#### In any case, if you’re experiencing any **issues playing video files in Karaoki** it’s time to install a [video codec pack](http://www.codecguide.com/download%5Fk-lite%5Fcodec%5Fpack%5Fmega.htm).

**Motion background karaoke video files** are becoming more sought after today by some karaoke hosts. The additional on-screen-visuals can **assist in creating a more unique and potentially exciting karaoke experience** for karaoke singers. Still, many KJs prefer the black and/or blue backgrounds of traditional mp3+G – siting that they are “less distracting for singers”.

The **K-Lite Mega Codec Pack** is touted by K-Lite as “The mega variant is the biggest of the four variants of the codec pack” – meaning the **free codec download pack** includes all the video codecs known to mankind.
