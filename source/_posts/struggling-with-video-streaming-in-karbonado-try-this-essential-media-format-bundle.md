Third-party media software can also overwrite the pre-loaded **video codecs** and completely prevent Karaoki from playing **karaoke video files** in general.

#### In any case, if you’re experiencing any **issues playing video files in Karaoki** it’s time to install a [video codec pack](http://www.codecguide.com/download%5Fk-lite%5Fcodec%5Fpack%5Fmega.htm).

The additional on-screen-visuals can **assist in creating a more unique and potentially exciting karaoke experience** for karaoke singers. Still, many KJs prefer the black and/or blue backgrounds of traditional mp3+G – siting that they are “less distracting for singers”.
