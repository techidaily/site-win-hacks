## DJ Christmas Essentials: Ultimate Shaders Collection for DEX 3 and LYRX Events | Seasonal Music Boosters

[![Holiday Shaders to use with DEX 3](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/holiday-shaders.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/holiday-shaders.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "Holiday Shaders to use with DEX 3")

**Spread a little holiday joy at your events this year with Christmas-themed and winter-themed Shaders!**

DEX 3 and LYRX allow DJs and karaoke hosts to use ‘[Shaders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shader)‘ to generate stunning on-screen visualizations that often dance to the beat of the music. Shaders are free to use from [ShaderToy.com](http://www.shadertoy.com) and are ‘drawn’ by your graphics card in real-time to generate beat-synced visualizations. Showcase the on your video/karaoke output display (or multiple displays) for your audience to view. Christmas-themed and winter-themed Shaders add to the ambiance of the event, ensuring party goers are kept in the holiday spirit of things.

![Shader Cube](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/shader-adjusted-1.png?resize=180%2C180&ssl=1 "Shader Cube")

[Installing Shaders for use in DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is easy, and they are _must-haves_ for those times you’re just mixing audio with [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/).

We’ve rounded up a few Christmas-themed and winter-themed Shaders list below that can be added to DEX 3 or LYRX. Simply copy the link/s of the Shader you wish to add below (right-click on link and ‘copy’) and add them in DEX 3 as instructed in the [video tutorial](https://youtu.be/jKDgSaaiJFE). (_**Pro Tip:** if you want to get a little more creative, [combine Shaders with Text, Image and Video overlays](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for multilayered goodness your fans will appreciate_.)
